Script

36 spoken lines and 16 short sounds (giggles, "Yes!", "Hi-yah!", barks). Plain everyday English, southern English family voices. The numbers match the recording script. In play, one voice at most every 10 to 15 seconds, tied to what just happened, never the same within 60 seconds, never over a finishing blow. Crumbs and Fluffini stay as speech bubbles and animal sounds.

Olive

The family's brown Staffordshire Bull Terrier. She follows Matilda, can't be hurt, never blocks or fights, and runs alongside or waits at the far end of rides.

  • Opening: at the party with Mummy and Leo, tail going; barks at Snuffles when he stomps in. He locks the front door and forgets the dog flap.
  • Early Street: squeezes out of the flap and catches up with a happy bark. Matilda: "Olive! Come on, girl!"
  • Her job: sniffing out hidden things. Near a hidden gift box or golden dahlia she stops, points, barks and digs at the spot. The first golden dahlia is taught this way, with no text.
  • Bosses: waits at the edge barking; a happy spin when each party thing comes back.
  • Ending: Snuffles gives her her own small slice of cake.
  • Art: none made yet. Once photos are in raw/olive/, I match her coat, white markings, ears, face and build, and show James the photo next to the game Olive before any animation. Up to 80 credits, listed per generation.

Voices

The AI audition is dropped; no more AI voices until James approves a new approach. Plan, in order:

  1. Real recordings with Voice Memos: James (Sensei, Snuffles), Autumn (Mummy, maybe Matilda), real children if possible (Matilda, Leo). Files into raw/voice/<character>/.
  2. If no child recording arrives for Matilda or Leo: Autumn's natural recording turned into a child's voice with the Higgsfield voice changer, keeping her timing. An audition of 3 conversions for James first. Up to 15 credits.
  3. Fewer words: Matilda speaks full lines only in the opening, at bosses, at Stunt Day and at the end; everywhere else short real sounds.

When files land: split the takes, keep the best by energy and clarity, gentle noise reduction, trim, level to −16 LUFS with peaks under −1 dB, wire in, and report the measured values. Credits so far: 4.5 (all on the dropped audition).
