Voices and loudness

raw/voice/ is empty, so Matilda's voice is an audition of three AI voices (A, B, C), the same three lines each, two takes per line, on the audition page. Every clip is levelled to −16 LUFS with peaks under −1 dB: the 18 Matilda clips measure −15.9 to −17.0 LUFS with peaks −1.6 to −5.9 dB; the 7 cast clips −16.4 to −17.8 LUFS (Snuffles' long line is quieter because its peaks hit the limit) with peaks −1.7 to −2.9 dB. Once a voice is chosen, every Matilda line in the script gets two takes, the best is kept by energy and clarity, and all are levelled the same way.

Credits this phase: 1.5 (9 second takes). Last round: 3.0. Total of the 40 allowed: 4.5.

Decisions for James

  1. Matilda's voice: A, B or C, and take 1 (bright) or take 2 (faster, higher).
  2. Mummy, Leo and Snuffles: keep these voices or try others.
  3. The opening storyboard: approve, or change any beat or line.
  4. The script: approve, or change any line. Proposed change: "Nobody ruins my party!" to "Nobody spoils my party!".
  5. The flagged fixes in the reason table: the sleeping first gull, the tiny first bunny, the tapping hand on STORM the first time, crates as party supply boxes, the combo counter only from 5 hits.
  6. Reference timings: match Mario for grow and shrink (2.8 s safe after a hit) as proposed, or keep a shorter safe time.
