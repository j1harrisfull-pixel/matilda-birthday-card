Recording script

For Matilda's birthday game. Everyday voices, like talking at home. Nothing posh, nothing like an advert.

MatildaLeoMummySenseiSnufflesOlive

How to record

  1. A quiet room with soft things in it. A bedroom with the curtains shut and a duvet is best. Turn off the TV, the washing machine and any fans.
  2. Use Voice Memos on the iPhone. If you can, first set Settings, Apps, Voice Memos, Audio Quality to Lossless.
  3. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, a little to one side so the "p" and "b" sounds don't pop.
  4. One Voice Memo per character. Rename it to the character, for example "Snuffles".
  5. Say the line number, then the line three times, with a short pause (about two seconds) between each one. Like: "M3. Olive! Come on, girl! ... Olive! Come on, girl! ... Olive! Come on, girl!"
  6. Talk normally. Say it the way you would at home. The three tries can be a bit different from each other, which helps.
  7. Record ten seconds first and listen back to check it is clear and not too loud.
  8. For children: read each line to them and let them say it back, as many tries as they like. Giggles and mistakes are fine; keep it a game and stop when they've had enough.
  9. Send them to the Mac: in Voice Memos tap the recording, tap the three dots, Share, AirDrop, then James's Mac. On the Mac it lands in Downloads. Drag each file into the matching folder in matilda-birthday/raw/voice/: matilda, leo, mummy, sensei, snuffles (and olive).
