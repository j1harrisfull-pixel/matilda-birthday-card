Playtest kit

For watching one child play Matilda's Black Belt Birthday from the start to the birthday card. Everything you tick is kept on this phone only (in this browser), so you can fill it in as you go.

Tester:

Rules for the grown up

  1. Open game-next.html?telemetry=1 on the phone, sound on, held sideways. Hand it over at the PLAY button.
  2. Say only: "This is a game. Have a go." Don't explain anything, don't point at the screen, don't touch the phone.
  3. If they ask for help, say "What do you think?" once. If they are still stuck after that, help, and tick asked for help.
  4. Watch their face as much as the screen. Tick a box the moment it happens. Write down exact words when they say something.
  5. Time it from PLAY to the birthday card.
  6. At the end, tap Save results on the summary and AirDrop the file to James's Mac (raw/telemetry/). Then ask the three questions below, in their words, no hints.

During play: one box per beat

Tick what happened in each part. Leave it empty if nothing did.

Afterwards: three questions

1. What were you trying to do in the game?
Pass: they say getting to her party (or the party, balloons, presents and cake). Not saving anyone.
2. Which button makes all the flowers come out?
Pass: they name or point to STORM.
3. What was your favourite bit, and was any bit boring or confusing?
Time from PLAY to the birthday card
Pass: under 6 minutes.

For James: a Safari timeline from the iPhone (5 steps)

  1. On the iPhone: Settings, Apps, Safari, Advanced, turn on Web Inspector.
  2. On the Mac: Safari, Settings, Advanced, tick Show features for web developers (this adds the Develop menu).
  3. Plug the iPhone into the Mac with a cable and open game-next.html?telemetry=1 in Safari on the phone.
  4. On the Mac: Develop, your iPhone's name, the game page. In the Web Inspector open Timelines, make sure Rendering Frames, CPU and Memory are on, press the red record button and play on the phone up to the end of the Crumbs fight.
  5. Stop recording, press Export (the arrow out of a box at the top of Timelines) and save the file into matilda-birthday/raw/telemetry/. Targets: no frame over 33 ms, memory under 200 MB.
