Opening storyboard

The opening as built in the test game, with speech bubbles (no voices). Each frame was taken the moment a bubble finished typing, in a run with no taps: every bubble waited its full reading time (1.2 s plus 0.3 s a word), so this is the timing for an average reader. A tap finishes a bubble early; the Skip button skips the whole opening. Olive is a placeholder shape until her photos arrive.
